# Jaydevsinh Zala — Executive Portfolio

Personal portfolio website for **Jaydevsinh Zala** (Researcher, Mechanical Engineer, Inventor, and Entrepreneur), showcasing applied research, publications, patents, ventures, experience, and credentials.

## Structure

- `index.html` — Semantic single-page portfolio with Open Graph, Twitter Card, and JSON-LD structured data
- `styles.css` — Dark/light theme tokens, executive typography (`Playfair Display` + `Inter`), and responsive grid layouts
- `script.js` — Theme persistence, intersection-observer scroll reveals, active navigation tracking, animated KPI counters, and email copy action
- `jaydev.jpeg` — Portrait image used in the hero and page favicon

## Sections

1. **Hero** — Profile introduction, location badge, LinkedIn/ORCID/Email links
2. **Metrics** — Engineering projects, published patents, research publications, ventures, and prototype grants
3. **About** — Executive profile, vision statement, and languages
4. **Core Competencies** — AI & Computer Vision (YOLO), IoT & Embedded Systems, CAD & Automation, Python, Applied Research & IP, Startup Leadership
5. **Ventures & Innovations** — Ananta Labs India and Spitting Detection System
6. **Research, Publications & Patent** — Published Indian/PCT patent details, ORCID link, and 3 research publications
7. **Experience & Education** — Chronological timeline of research, industry, leadership, and academic milestones
8. **Credentials & Honors** — Awards, certifications, grants/incubation, and professional memberships (IMRTC, IFERP, IEEE, ISEA, IAENG)
9. **Contact & Location** — Direct email (with one-click copy), LinkedIn, ORCID, and Vadodara location map
